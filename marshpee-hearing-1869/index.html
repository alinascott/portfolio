HOUSE......(SUPPLEMENT TO HOUSE No. 483.)......No. 502.

HEARING

before the

COMMITTEE ON INDIANS,

AT MARSHPEE,
TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 9, 1869.
Phonographically Reported by James H. Slade.

On February 9, 1869, a joint committee of the Massachusetts legislature met the people of the Marshpee District at the meeting-house to hear arguments for and against lifting the restriction (the "entailment") that kept their lands from being sold to outsiders, and over whether residents should become full citizens. A stenographer took down the speeches, and the State printed them in June as House Document No. 502.

The full text below is transcribed from the State Library of Massachusetts scan. The original spelling and punctuation are kept, and the printed page numbers are marked.

  • Original spelling, punctuation, capitalization and italics are kept, including "seive" (p. 7), "pretent" (p. 27), "Gouch" (p. 25) and "Mr. Sewall Fessenden" (p. 14).
  • Where a paragraph runs over a page, the new page number is marked inside the paragraph.
  • Running heads are omitted. The printer's signature marks (2–5) at the foot of pp. 9, 17, 25 and 33 are noted at those page ends.
  • The title page (p. 1) and blank verso (p. 2) are reproduced in the header above.
